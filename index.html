VeloMeter

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the VeloMeter app for Wear OS ("VeloMeter", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. VeloMeter is developed and published on Google Play by Velometer. If you have any questions, contact us at velometervbt@gmail.com.

The short version
  • Everything the app records stays on your watch.
  • The app has no internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising ID.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

1. Information the app uses

VeloMeter measures how fast a barbell moves. To do that, it reads the watch's motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope and rotation sensor), but only while you are recording a set. It does not use location, the microphone, the camera, contacts, heart rate or any other health sensor.

The app stores the following information only on your watch:

InformationWhy
Your sets: date and time, number of reps, mean and peak velocity of each rep, and the exercise nameTo show your results and your History
Exercise names you add yourselfSo you can pick them again later
The motion sensor recording of each setSo a set's numbers can be checked and the rep detection improved
A CSV file of your history (velometer_history.csv)So you can copy your data off the watch if you want to
Your settings: accent colour and whether to suggest the exercise after a setTo remember your preferences

This information is kept in the app's private storage on the watch. Because the app does not request internet access, none of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else. App data is also excluded from Android cloud backup.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data through the app. We have no servers that receive data from the app, and the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

3. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any user data with third parties, because we do not have it. The only way data leaves your watch is if you copy it yourself, for example by transferring the CSV file to a computer. What happens to that copy is up to you.

4. Typing or speaking an exercise name

When you add a custom exercise name, the app opens the standard Wear OS input screen. If you use voice input there, the speech is handled by your watch's system input service (usually Google), not by VeloMeter, under that provider's own privacy policy. VeloMeter only receives the resulting text and saves it on the watch.

5. Purchases

If VeloMeter is a paid app, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google provides developers with standard sales reports, such as an order number, price and country, which we use only for accounting and refunds. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems you report. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we will delete your messages if you ask us to.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your watch until you delete it. You can:

  • delete a single set from the History screen, which also removes it from the CSV file;
  • delete everything, including the sensor recordings, by clearing the app's storage in the watch's settings (Settings → Apps → VeloMeter → Storage → Clear data); or
  • uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from the watch.

Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it once it is deleted.

8. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. It is never sent over a network, so it cannot be intercepted in transit or leaked from a server. Keep your watch protected with a screen lock to stop other people from viewing it.

9. Children

VeloMeter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since all app data is on your own watch, you can see and delete it there at any time. For any request about emails you have sent us, contact velometervbt@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released. If an update ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the app's Google Play data safety section first.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: velometervbt@gmail.com
